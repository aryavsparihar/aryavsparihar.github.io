About

I am a Computer Science undergraduate at Shri Mata Vaishno Devi University. My research centers on the physical deployment of foundation models at the edge of autonomous and embodied systems. I study hardware-software co-design strategies that compress, adapt, and localize AI models onto microcontroller-class and single-board platforms, enabling real-time, cloud-independent inference for robotics and human-robot interaction.

My work spans the full stack, from model compression (quantization, pruning, distillation) and edge-native sensor fusion to perception-action pipelines for autonomous agents in dynamic, GPS-denied environments. Increasingly, I am interested in the robustness, security, and interpretability of these systems. I am currently an Erasmus+ R&D intern at the University of Ljubljana, and I founded Project Nida-e-Watan, a digital-inclusion initiative in the Chenab Valley.

Joining NTU Singapore in January 2027

I will be a Visiting Researcher at the Cognitive Interface Lab, Nanyang Technological University, working on edge-deployed foundation models for embodied AI.

Behaviour-Based Detection of USB Charging Threats

May 2026 – Jul 2026

A detection pipeline and telemetry simulator for malicious USB charging sessions. Testing is deliberately hard: on chargers and devices the model never saw, recall at a 1% false-positive budget is 80%, with large variance across seeds.
