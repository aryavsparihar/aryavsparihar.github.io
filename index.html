Selected projects

2026

Behaviour-Based Detection of USB Charging Threats

A detection pipeline and telemetry simulator for juice jacking, HID input injection, class-swapping devices, and concealed hardware. The simulator generates 60,000 sessions with 82 features across 14 attack and benign families. The detector is tested under deliberately hard conditions: unseen devices and chargers, held-out attack families, benign look-alikes, and a realistic 0.1% attack rate. On hardware the model never saw, recall at a 1% false-positive budget is 80% (with high variance across seeds). Accuracy on familiar hardware is 98%, against 77% for a rule baseline. The scorer exports to 27 KB of dependency-free C at about 1 µs p99 latency. Results are on synthetic data, and the project includes an audit that retracted an earlier version's inflated numbers.

Gradient boosting · SHAP · ONNX · C export · adversarial evaluation

Nida-e-Watan: Offline Edge Inference Cluster

An offline-first AI system across 12 Raspberry Pi 4 nodes serving villages with about two hours of connectivity a day. Quantizing and pruning multilingual BERT and IndicBERT cut the model from 1.1 GB to 260 MB while keeping 91% task accuracy. Classification runs in 380 ms on device, and CRDT-based sync keeps 99.2% data integrity between uplink windows.

Raspberry Pi · ONNX Runtime · quantization · pruning · CRDTs

2026

Multispectral Terrain Classification & Multi-Agent Routing

A spectral ablation study in which a ResNet-18 on five bands (RGB + NIR + SWIR1) beat RGB alone, 97.76% vs 97.06%. Multi-seed cross-validation tested whether the gain was real, and Grad-CAM showed what the extra bands changed. The classified terrain then fed a multi-objective A* router. There, a heavy convoy, light recon, and stealth infantry agent re-plan around soil moisture, viewshed, and a simulated rainfall event.

PyTorch · ResNet-18 · Grad-CAM · A* search

2026

Eco-Smart Wearable Biosensing Patch

A cloud-free biosensing patch on an Arduino Nano, with electrochemical electrodes and temperature sensors. A time-series classifier runs on the microcontroller itself, within its SRAM and flash limits, to flag physiological anomalies in real time.

Arduino Nano · PyTorch · TinyML · biosignals
